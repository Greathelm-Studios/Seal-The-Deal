On technical-only choices (netcode stack, transports, architecture), the agent leads: make the call, record it in [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md), and explain in one line what it changes in the game.

**Why:** 2026-09-28, Adam on FishNet vs Netcode for GameObjects: "netcode isnt my skill so i cant tell you anymore than you can tell me ... ill follow your lead for that". Since 2026-11-22 design is no longer Adam's alone but a group decision (Harvey).

**How to apply:** Decide technical questions yourself and report the decision plus any gameplay consequence. Anything that changes design or feel (weapons, layout, rules, scope) goes to the team through your human (AGENTS.md §1, §7).
