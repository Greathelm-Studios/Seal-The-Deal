Weapon models look like **simple, clean, low-poly stylised real guns in flat colours**: believable shapes with simplified detail, a few flat colour blocks and no grime.

On 2026-11-20 and 21, Alex (environment artist) turned down every other direction for the regular pistol:
- round 1 concepts, "too toyish": stubby, pillowy and glossy;
- round 2, "too realistic": serrations, levers, screws and textured grips;
- a sleek "smart pistol" product look, scripted in Blender, "too blocky / simple" and the "wrong shape";
- a sleek sci-fi pistol (PX-24 on Sketchfab), "too sci fi".

He then picked three Sketchfab models as the style: "Stylized Handgun" by Yurrpo, "Stylized pistol" by vit4ce, and "Guns Low poly" by Satendra Saraswat. All three are CC-BY, and their sheets are in `ArtSource/Weapons/concepts/Pistol/`.

**Why:** five rounds of concepts and scripted models missed. Ready-made low-poly flat-colour guns were the first thing he liked.

**How to apply:**
- Start weapons from a model in this style, ready-made or matched to these picks, rather than painted concepts or scripted sculpting.
- Recolour to the office palette (art bible), keeping the flat colours.
- Keep every CC-BY credit line for the game's credits.
- Ask Alex before changing the style.
