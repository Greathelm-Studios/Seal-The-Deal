# Office look, kit pipeline, and animated shaders

**Status:** Current in KitOffice and KitTest (not measured for frame cost in multiplayer) · **Last updated:** 2026-11-21

Seal the Deal takes place in crisp white, glass, and cobalt Japanese corporate offices lit by a diagonal afternoon sun under a deep blue sky.
Sunlight casts soft-edged geometric patches across polished tiled floors, warm light pools highlight conference rooms and desks against cooler corridors, glass partitions carry subtle frosted dot bands, and decorative screens, tickers, dust motes, and water features keep rooms alive without any networked state.

## Architecture and rationale

### Scene lighting and grade (`OfficeLook`)

- **Editor-applied scene data over runtime overrides:** [OfficeLook.cs](../../Assets/_Project/Editor/Art/OfficeLook.cs) (`Close the Deal > Art > Apply Office Look`) writes `RenderSettings`, the directional sun, and a global URP `Volume` in the editor rather than applying values on `Awake`, preserving authored scene overrides (`AGENTS.md §4`). Generated assets ([OfficeLook_Volume.asset](../../Assets/_Project/Art/Look/OfficeLook_Volume.asset) and [OfficeLook_Sky.mat](../../Assets/_Project/Art/Look/OfficeLook_Sky.mat)) are protected by `GeneratedAssetGuard` and updated in place so scene GUID references remain intact.
- **Neutral tonemapping and controlled white-on-cool contrast:** URP `Neutral` tonemapping is used instead of `ACES` because `ACES` rolls whites toward grey and pushes blues toward purple, fighting the Paper White and cobalt palette. Vignette, chromatic aberration, lens distortion, and motion blur are deliberately omitted to keep the corporate aesthetic clean.
- **Sun geometry and shadow filtering:** Elevation `32` deg and heading `140` deg send sunlight diagonally across the street through `4.3 m` windows about `5 m` onto the office floor (heading `80` deg previously ran parallel to the windows and lit no interiors; `40` deg elevation left only a narrow strip under the sill). Because one sun cannot light opposing street facades simultaneously, the `+Z`-facing tower receives direct interior sun while the opposite tower sits in shade. Sun shadows use `LightShadows.Soft` with `SoftShadowQuality.Low` rather than hard shadows to blur single-texel stair-stepping without losing crisp architectural edges.
- **Exposure and bloom balance:** Post-exposure `+1.0` stops lifts white and pearl surfaces cleanly without clipping (`1.25` clipped pearl props; `0.85` read dull grey), while `Bloom` threshold `1.5` and intensity `0.2` restrict glow to genuine highlights (LED strips, sun glints) rather than blooming sunlit walls and floors. Sky and linear fog (`80 m` to `700 m`, matching the sky horizon colour `#9EE0FC`) receive `SkyExposureComp` so the sky holds `SkyShownExposure` (`0.6` stops) instead of washing white. `DynamicGI.UpdateEnvironment()` is invoked immediately because the Editor only refreshes environment probes automatically when Auto Generate is enabled.
- **Bounce lighting (`BounceLight`):** URP real-time lighting has no GI bounce, leaving ceilings unlit over sun patches. [BounceLight.cs](../../Assets/_Project/Editor/Art/BounceLight.cs) (`Close the Deal > Art > Add Bounce Light`) projects the sun vector in `Kit Office` local space through each `10 x 4 m` facade bay and places shadowless upward spotlights (`Intensity = 2.5`, `Range = 7 m`, `OuterAngle = 160` deg, `InnerAngle = 40` deg, cooled tint `(0.86, 0.91, 1)`) `0.15 m` above each floor patch instead of Adaptive Probe Volumes, avoiding mixed-lighting bakes per stacked floor. See [FLOOR_REFLECTIONS.md](FLOOR_REFLECTIONS.md) for the lighting/probe run order.

### Modular kit import (`KitImport`)

- **FBX import and collider classification:** [KitImport.cs](../../Assets/_Project/Editor/Art/KitImport.cs) (`Close the Deal > Art > Import Kit and Build Test Scene`) imports pieces exported from `ArtSource/Kit/export` at `globalScale = 1` with `useFileUnits = true` and `bakeAxisConversion = false` (Blender exports already bake axes/units), validates bounds against `manifest.json` within `1 mm`, creates prefabs under `Assets/_Project/Art/Kit/Prefabs/`, and runs collider seam raychecks against `KitTest.unity`. For `*_Collider` sub-meshes, `IsAxisAlignedBox` verifies every vertex lies on the bounds faces (because sloped stair guards are also 12-triangle hulls) before emitting a cheaper `BoxCollider`; other colliders use convex `MeshCollider`s, `VisualOnly` pieces omit colliders, and `Slab_Stairwell`/`Stair_Flight` use render meshes as non-convex colliders.
- **Materials and trim sheet:** Textured kit materials (`Carpet_Dark`, `Ceiling_Grid`, `Floor_Carpet`, `Glass_Fluted`, `Glass_Louvre`) clear or apply `_BaseMap` with white tint so textures are not darkened twice. `Glass_Louvre` uses emissive texture glow (`(0.3, 0.55, 1.3)`) so backlit slats read as lit from blue rooms behind them. Prop acrylic (`Plastic_Aqua_Frost`, `Plastic_Frost_Pale`) uses alpha `0.82` over `Glass_Frosted`'s `0.65` so aqua props do not wash grey. `Trim_Aero` combines `ArtSource/Kit/trim/build/export` PBR maps (`AlbedoTransparency`, `MetallicSmoothness`, `Normal`, `Emission` at `1.5` HDR multiplier) with `Close the Deal/Aero Plastic`.

### Review office scene (`KitOfficeScene`)

- **L2 twin-core court layout:** [KitOfficeScene.cs](../../Assets/_Project/Editor/Art/KitOfficeScene.cs) (`Close the Deal > Art > Build Kit Office Scene`) lays out `KitOffice.unity` on a `40 x 32 m` (`10 x 8` bays of `4 m`, snapped to a `2 m` grid) two-storey plate (`FloorHeight = 4.5 m`) driven by `ArtSource/Kit/markings/l2_on_kit.json`. Plan coordinates map to Unity as `X = x, Z = -y`, with firm B mirrored `180` deg around the plate centre except for the north-facade lift (`x 18..22`) and the court feature stair (`Stair_Straight_2m`). Two storeys are built instead of three to match the `9 m` travel of `KitOfficeRoof`.
- **Clipping and fit-out rules:** `StairHole` starts at `x = 11.8` rather than `12` so upper slabs clear the `2 m` return stair stringers; wall runs with `trimA`/`trimB` scale end pieces at curtain walls and window sills instead of adding custom short kit pieces; south corners place inverted `Column_CornerL`/`Column_CornerR` to prevent overlapping mullions; wall-adjacent furniture sits `0.03 m` off walls; `PC_Tower` sits beside bench pedestals outside end panels so it does not clip panels or knee space.
- **Light pools and Mood D colour:** Shadowless spotlights (`RoomPoolIntensity = 6`, `WashIntensity = 5`, `PlinthIntensity = 14`) illuminate rooms, desks, lightboxes, and the court plinth/waterfall while leaving corridors darker. Firm halves carry cobalt (`HalfALight`) and azure (`HalfBLight`) pools and painted wall fields (`4 mm` off walls, clearing `3 mm` wall joints and sitting behind `6 mm` signs/decals).
- **Build pipeline, additive captures, and FishNet IDs:** `BuildAndSave` places the play rig (`GreyboxSceneSetup.EnsurePlayRig`), applies `OfficeLook`, builds `KitOfficeAnimation`, places `BounceLight`, hides `Markings`, `Animated`, and `CRT` overlays before baking `FloorReflections` and `ZoneReflections` (avoiding baked cyan casts and frozen additive glow), runs `KitOfficeRoof.AddTo`, and calls `GiveNetworkSceneIds` (reflecting FishNet's internal `CreateSceneId`, `RemoveDuplicateNetworkObjects`, and `ReserializeEditorSetValues`, plus assigning `DefaultPrefabObjects.asset` so scene `NetworkObject`s such as the lift are not disabled on Play). `BuildAdditive` snapshots the active scene look into a temporary additive scene and discards one warm-up render before capturing review shots because a camera's first render over newly built objects previously drew the floor below through carpet overlays.

### Signs and decals (`KitSigns`)

- **Textured quads over URP Decal Projectors:** [KitSigns.cs](../../Assets/_Project/Editor/Art/KitSigns.cs) places signs, prop labels, glazing dot manifestations (`Partition_Glass_*`), floor/soffit inlay markings, and satirical wall text as `PrimitiveType.Quad` meshes (`1` draw call per material, no extra URP Renderer Feature, exact flat wall alignment). Quads face `-normal` via `Facing` because Unity's built-in `Quad` faces local `-Z`.
- **Offsets and material finishes:** Wall decals stand off `6 mm` (`WallGap`), piece labels `4 mm` (`LabelGap`), clearing `3 mm` wall joint geometry without visible gaps. `Finish.Printed` uses alpha blending with specular highlights and environment reflections disabled so transparent regions do not render as pale glossy boxes over walls; `Finish.Inlay` (smoothness `0.96`) and `Finish.Light` (`LightEmission = 1.6`) use alpha clipping (`_Cutoff = 0.5`) with `mipMapsPreserveCoverage = true` so thin floor and ceiling markings do not vanish at distance.

### Local decorative animation (`KitOfficeAnimation`, `OfficeAnimationOwnership`, `OfficeLouvreDrift`)

- **Local-only ownership:** All decorative office animation is local to each client with zero networking or synced state.
- **Guarded rebuilds:** [KitOfficeAnimation.cs](../../Assets/_Project/Editor/Art/KitOfficeAnimation.cs) records a JSON/reference hash in [OfficeAnimationOwnership.cs](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Art/OfficeAnimationOwnership.cs) (stripping volatile `instanceID`s while hashing asset GUID/local IDs and hierarchy paths) covering both `Kit Office/Animated` and monitor-parented `Animated CRT` overlays. Rebuilds check all shader/texture dependencies before destroying an old rig and abort if a designer has modified any generated object. Decorative renderers disable static batching (`SetStaticEditorFlags(geometry, 0)`), shadows, probes, and motion vectors so world-origin shader phases work.
- **Analytic sun cookie drift:** [OfficeLouvreDrift.cs](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Art/OfficeLouvreDrift.cs) reads the sun's authored `lightCookieOffset` in `OnEnable` (only when `Application.isPlaying`), oscillates it in `LateUpdate` along local Y (`swayMetres = 0.12`, `swaySeconds = 30`) in URP projection metres, and restores the authored offset on `OnDisable` only if no other tool replaced the cookie or offset.

### Art shaders (`Art/Animated`, `Art/Plastic`, `Art/Water`)

- **Animated shaders (`Assets/_Project/Art/Animated/`):**
  - `OfficeCrt.shader` (`Close the Deal/Animated/Office CRT`): Unlit scrolling spreadsheet/chart/document screens with `DisableBatching = True` so each monitor hashes its world origin into an independent page/scroll phase without per-screen materials or property blocks; scanlines fade out with `fwidth(uv.y)` to prevent distant moire.
  - `OfficeSlides.shader` (`Close the Deal/Animated/Office Slides`): Plays an 8-slide (`4 x 2`) conference deck from `_BaseMap` with `DisableBatching = True` and an affine world-position phase keeping both ground-floor conference rooms out of step. Samples at explicit mip `0` with half-texel tile inset so bilinear filtering and mipmaps never bleed neighbouring slides.
  - `OfficeTicker.shader` (`Close the Deal/Animated/Office Ticker`): Steps `_TextMask` horizontally across a discrete `_DotsAcross x _DotsHigh` LED lattice with edge fade.
  - `OfficeShaft.shader` (`Close the Deal/Animated/Office Shaft`): Crossed additive planes (`DisableBatching = True`) with breathing wisps, edge-on view fade (`facing`), and camera near-fade (`0.25 m` to `1.0 m`) so crossed planes never read as hard cards.
  - `OfficeDust.shader` (`Close the Deal/Animated/Office Dust`): Soft additive particle billboards driven by `ParticleSystem` vertex colours with `0.3 m` to `1.0 m` camera near-fade (`3` systems x `64` particles max in `KitOffice`).
- **Moulded plastic shader (`Assets/_Project/Art/Plastic/AeroPlastic.shader`):**
  - `Close the Deal/Aero Plastic` extends URP Lit (`_CLEARCOAT` always defined, controlled by `_ClearCoatMask`) with a top-to-underside studio gradient toward `AERO_UNDERSIDE` `(0.80, 0.85, 0.92)` and an ambient-scaled Fresnel rim (`SampleSH`).
  - Reuses existing URP Lit `UnityPerMaterial` fields (`_SpecColor` for rim colour/strength, `_DetailAlbedoMapScale` for rim tightness, `_DetailNormalMapScale` for underside gradient strength) instead of declaring custom properties so every pass keeps URP Lit's buffer layout and stays SRP Batcher compatible.
- **Water shader (`Assets/_Project/Art/Water/AeroWater.shader`):**
  - `Close the Deal/Aero Water Pane` draws an interior-mapped tank behind `Water_Wall` panes (`Cull Back` on closed boxes so back faces do not double alpha) and switches to `PoolTop` mode on upward-facing surfaces (`normalWS.y >= 0.9`) for `Pool_Raised_8x4`.
  - Computes view-ray parallax into `_TankDepth` (clamping inward component at `MIN_INWARD = 0.25` to prevent grazing smearing), dual Voronoi caustic layers, swaying light shafts, two depth-sorted bubble layers, `sqrt(depth01)` colour absorption so shallow water transitions cleanly under bright post-exposure, the silvery surface underside when looking up, and sliding glass streaks.

## Known limitations

- **Single sun direction:** Only the `+Z`-facing facade receives direct interior sun patches; opposing towers stay in ambient/bounce shade.
- **FishNet reflection dependency:** `KitOfficeScene.GiveNetworkSceneIds` relies on internal `NetworkObject` methods (`CreateSceneId`, `RemoveDuplicateNetworkObjects`, `ReserializeEditorSetValues`) via reflection and throws `MissingMethodException` if a FishNet update renames them.
- **Review scenes only:** `KitOffice.unity` and `KitTest.unity` are look-dev and assembly review scenes; live match levels are built through the tower/integration pipeline.
- **Not measured in full 8-player Play Mode:** Shader and shadowless light-pool frame budgets (around 40 pools + 21 bounce spots + `AeroWater`/`OfficeSky`) have not been profiled in live multiplayer.
