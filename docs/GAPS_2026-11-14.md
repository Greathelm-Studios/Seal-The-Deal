## Questions for Adam (recommended answer first)

1. **Is the briefcase mode the game now?** The GDD has no briefcase: it says first team to the roof wins, checkpoints every 3 floors, a boardroom fight, window exits to the checkpoint. `buffer` plays one shared briefcase, floor gates, skybridges, no boardroom. *Recommend:* yes, and the GDD's core loop is rewritten to match (only you edit the GDD).
2. **Gates or breakable doors?** Both hold a team on a floor today (MATCH.md known limitations). *Recommend:* keep the gates, drop doors from v1 scope.
3. **Product name, company and bundle id in the build.** ProjectSettings say `productName: CloseTheDeal`, `companyName: DefaultCompany`, bundle id from Unity's URP template, and the Unity splash on. The window title, the save folder and Steam all read these. *Recommend:* "Seal the Deal" and your studio name (Greathelm Studios?), splash off, before the first build anyone else sees. 4. **Firm names.** Lobby says Cobalt / Cyan, match says Ashcombe & Pryce / Halloran Voss (INTEGRATION.md). *Recommend:* the law-firm names everywhere.
5. **Price.** GDD leaves it open; Oro's range is £5–£10. The paid friend-group hits sit at £6–£9 (Lethal Company £8.50, Content Warning ~£6.39, Bopl Battle £8.50); the physics brawlers at ~£15.50 (market section). *Recommend:* £7.99 / $9.99, and only once the second-session hooks below and a full 8-player test exist.
6. **What counts as a win stat when a player joined late or left before the end.** *Recommend (applied, reversible):* a finished match counts for everyone in it at the end; a player who left before the end gets nothing written; a late joiner gets a full record of what they did.
7. **Release dates.** The GDD's 7 Oct store page and 29 Oct release have passed. AGENTS.md §2 still says they "cannot move". Both need new dates or a note that they moved.
8. **Third-party content on Alex's and Harvey's branches** (raised by Harvey's session, #457): the Kenney Blaster Kit and MrEliptik Office Pack under `Assets/ThirdParty`, and five Kevin Iglesias run clips that CHARACTER_ANIMATION.md says are not cleared for a public repository. The GDD says no store pack ships. *Recommend:* check each licence; keep CC0 packs (credit them anyway), replace anything not cleared before the store page, and add a credits screen.
9. **Accidental files on Alex's branch** (#457): render caches, `.pyc` files and multi-thousand-line logs under `ArtSource/Kit`. *Recommend:* remove and gitignore them before anything merges into `main`.
10. **Names in reception.** Names over heads follow the research note: your own firm only. *Recommend:* keep that in matches; say if reception, a social room, should show everyone's name.
11. **Career stats are beyond the GDD's v1 list**, which has only "results screen and rematch" (AGENTS.md §2). You asked for "things the game would need", so this lane built them; say if they should wait.

### Assumptions this lane made (Adam can overrule)

- **The profile holds career stats and onboarding flags only.** The outfit stays in the lobby's `outfit.json` and settings stay with lane 5, so no value lives in two files.
- **Only a finished match counts.** Each machine writes its own profile only when the host's final record for it arrives; a host leaving mid-match writes nothing. A client never counts its own stats.
- **No spectating in v1** (row 8): 5 s deaths are covered by the death card; nothing was built, so there is nothing to undo.
- **Everything installs itself** into scenes with a match from one prefab, so no scene file was edited and a scene rebuild can't drop it. Deleting `Resources/Gaps/RecordsInstaller.asset` turns it off.

- **The money is in £6–£9 friend-group games**, and $7.99–$9.99 is the common indie price ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/game-pricing-indie-steam-peak-content-warning-2000658886)).
- **Two teams need more friends than co-op does.** Lethal Company and Content Warning need 4 players; a good Seal the Deal match wants 4–8. Multiplayer-only games die when the pool is too small to fill games: Decksplash peaked at 464 concurrent in a free week and was stopped ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/decksplash/decksplash-free-weekend-players)). Private Steam lobbies and invites are the whole funnel, so 2v2 (or 1v1) must feel good.
- **Would it stand next to them today? No.** It has the hook (the briefcase, the towers, the secretary) but no reason to come back, no voice during play, no results worth screenshotting and no build a stranger can install. **The biggest gap is the second session**: rows 1, 2, 5 and 6 above, which is why this lane builds them first.
