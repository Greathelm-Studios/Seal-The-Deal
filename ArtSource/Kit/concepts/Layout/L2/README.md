**Agency lessons:** open Court contrasts with dense office wings; Window Hall, Court and Copy / Archive give three route choices; real room functions supply callouts; reception lightboxes, the feature flight and upper glass bridge supply landmarks.
The room route rejoins the ring instead of ending in a back room, and office storage returns break aligned cross-floor shots.
This applies the structure described in [BRIEF_L2](../BRIEF_L2.md), without copying Agency geometry or importing its hostage objectives; no extra deep-wing objective is invented.
