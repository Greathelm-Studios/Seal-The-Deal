# Office floor layout: L2 approved

**Date:** 2026-11-15 · **Decided by:** Alex ("this works") · **Applies to:** [L2/plan.png](L2/plan.png), [L2/flow.png](L2/flow.png), [L2/stack.png](L2/stack.png), [BRIEF_L2.md](BRIEF_L2.md)

## Decision

**L2, the twin-core court**, is the office floor layout. It replaces the current KitOffice labyrinth arrangement (see [CURRENT_DIAGNOSIS.md](CURRENT_DIAGNOSIS.md) for why). The visual direction is unchanged: DECISION_r5 plus the AEROSET and PANLINE notes, and signage as textures or decals.

In short, a 40 × 32 m floor:
- a central double-height **court** (feature stair, briefcase start, upper glass bridge, solid canopy)
- the **lift lobby** opening straight onto the court
- **two rotated cores**, each with an enclosed stair (E1, E2) and live rooms on its faces (print and copy, kitchen, coffee kitchen, server and archive)
- a continuous **ring**
- **dense named office wings** east and west, with window halls north and south
- **firms in opposite corners** and three named routes (Window Hall, Court, Copy + Archive)
- every room with two exits

The shell (cores, stairs, court and lift) repeats on every floor; each floor's fit-out varies.

## Still open

- **Long diagonals:** the five longest sightlines are 32–35 m, corner to corner past the court. Consider screen walls or columns at the court corners to break them for close-quarters play. Not yet decided.
- **One building or two towers:** the game mode on main (`docs/systems/MATCH.md`) is still built as two towers with a plaza and skybridges; Alex wants one large office. L2 works for either (gate lines at every stair arrival, a marked skybridge port). **Needs Alex and Adam.**
- **Building it:** nothing in Unity, the kit or KitOfficeScene.cs has changed yet.
