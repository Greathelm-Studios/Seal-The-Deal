## What changes

1. **Two or three distinct design directions per piece**, each with a one-line idea ("the façade reads as dark horizontal bands"). Not variations on one colour.
2. **Recesses are unlimited.** Reveals, shadow gaps, slots and chamfers may cut as deep into the envelope as the design wants, as long as no snap face moves and the sill stays solid cover. Proud detail stays at 15 mm or less.
3. **Character comes from shape and colour blocking**, the way the reference plates do it: lit slots, deep reveals, clean radiused or chamfered edges, big cobalt or navy blocks. Still no greeble.
4. **New material slots are allowed as proposals.** If a direction needs a cobalt accent, a navy trim or an emissive light material, use it and name it in the notes (e.g. `Accent_Cobalt`, `Trim_Navy`, `Light_Strip`). Alex decides which get added.
5. **Shaded renders, not diagrams.** For each direction:
   - **Hero shot:** room side at 1.7 m eye height, three bays in a row, soft cool daylight, bright interior.
   - **Street shot:** the façade seen from the rival tower 12 m away, three bays over two floors, with a navy figure behind the glass.
   - **Turnaround page:** keep front, side, three-quarter and section with sizes marked, as in round 1, but shaded.
   A throwaway Blender scene is the easiest way. Keep its script in the piece's folder.

## Starting points for the window bay (take them, mix them, or beat them)

- **Dark bands.** Navy sill-top and head bands that run across the column faces on the street side, so the tower reads as white floors striped with dark window bands (bible §09). Frameless glass between. White columns with a deep vertical reveal on each side.
- **Lit piers.** Columns with a full-height recessed light slot on the room face, as in the reference plates. Radiused outer corners on the columns. Silver sill cap with a shadow gap under it.
- **Cobalt pier.** One big graphic move: the street face of a column split diagonally white and cobalt. Too loud on every column, so show it on corner columns only, or every third bay, and say which.
