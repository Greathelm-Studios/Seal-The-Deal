## What Alex's references share (build from this, not from round 1)

- **White is the canvas; each zone owns ONE bold, saturated accent, and it arrives as light.** Coloured light floods white surfaces and spills on the floor (`03_green_division`, `06_blue_atrium`). The accent also takes whole surfaces: an end wall, a bumper rail, a floor field, a steel stair (`07`, `08`, `09`, `10` in orange).
- **Real value contrast.** Dark planes against white: a dark glossy floor reflecting light boxes (`13`), a black wall panel in a white lobby (`01`), a deep night-blue room (`14`), a dark atrium with one glowing zone (`04`). The current build is white on white with no darks.
- **Giant graphics.** Numbers and words metres tall on walls and glass (`02` digits on fluted glass, `03` "20 / DIVISION"), and big abstract wall art (`05`). Never small signs.
- **Hero objects and materials.** A tree and a waterfall in the lobby (`01`), sphere pendants (`04`, `05`), a sculptural or glass stair (`11`), fluted frosted glass (`02`), pillowed curved walls with cove light (`12`), backlit light boxes (`13`), glowing CRTs (`14`).
- **Directional light with shape.** Light through louvres or slats casts stripes (`03`); skylights cast blue shafts (`06`).

These are **style references only**: invent everything. Copy no logos, text, characters or exact designs from them. Our own brand is AONAGI (an open aqua ring with a red dot) and the firms Ashcombe & Pryce and Halloran Voss.

## Direction D: Colour-coded

Bright day, Mirror's-Edge-style clarity, our own design. White glossy canvas, and **each zone floods with one accent**:
- **Firm A's half (west wings, window hall north, reception NW): orange.** Orange end walls, bumper rails on corridor walls, orange light spilling through the frosted glass of its rooms, giant orange floor numbers.
- **Firm B's half (east): green**, treated the same way.
- **The court and the lift lobby: cool blue light from above**, falling in shafts onto the closing table; a tree or a slim indoor waterfall as the court's hero object (keep it clear of the routes).
- **The service cores and enclosed stairs: orange steel** (the utility look of `08`).
Add one giant graphic per view (a floor number, a district letter or a word) and fluted frosted glass on the room walls.

## Direction E: Night tech

The same office after dark, cool and graphic (`13`, `14`, `04`, `12`):
- **Dark glossy floors** reflecting light boxes, screens and cove lines; deep night-blue ambient.
- **White surfaces glowing from their own light**: cove-lit pillowed walls, backlit light boxes, glowing CRT screens and desk panels.
- **One accent colour (electric cyan-blue) used sparingly**, plus a single warm note at the reception.
- **The court as the brightest place**: a lit volume with the closing table spotlit and the feature stair edge-lit.
- Players (the dark capsules) must still read against the darks: rim them with the light behind.

## Rules

- **Paint over each capture: keep the camera, layout, walls, doors, stairs, glass, cover heights and routes exactly.** Change light, colour, materials, graphics, set dressing and atmosphere only. A hero object (tree, waterfall, sphere pendant) may stand in the court only where nothing walkable is.
- **No bubbles, no ivy, no cyan floor light trails** (floor *reflections* of lights are fine). No dirt or decay; no sci-fi greeble.
- Signage stays graphic (painted, printed, applied vinyl or backlit), never extruded 3D letters.
