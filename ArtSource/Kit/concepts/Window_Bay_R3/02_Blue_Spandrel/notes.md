# 02 / Blue Spandrel

**Status:** Target concept for Alex's review · **Last updated:** 2026-11-11

The tower reads as tall clear windows above continuous, glossy cobalt ribbons.

## The large move

The entire 1.00 m street face of the solid sill becomes opaque backpainted glass in Corporate Cobalt #244FB9.
The same flush finish crosses the first metre of each column's street face, so this is a floor-wide band rather than isolated coloured infill panels.
The original white 0.20 m slab edge separates floors; upper piers stay Paper White.
The colour covers an architectural mass; it is not a logo, stripe, or small applied accent.

## Build dimensions and materials

| Part | Treatment | Material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sill | Full original solid 4/2 m × 0.20 m × 1.00 m box; front face gets a flush finish assignment | White room face; proposed `Glass_Spandrel` on street face |
| Spandrel finish | Cobalt #244FB9; opaque, glossy, conceptual backpainted glass; no extra pane thickness | Proposed `Glass_Spandrel` |
| Column | Original 0.40/0.42 × 0.24 × 4.30 m; street finish changes at exactly 1.00 m | Existing white above; proposed spandrel below |
| Rails | Original 50 × 100 mm rail section and fixed elevations | A: existing `Frame_Aluminium`; B: proposed `Trim_Navy` |
| Clear pane | Original 3.60/1.60 × 3.20 × 0.02 m; separate from opaque spandrel | Existing `Glass_Clear` slot, concept shader |
| Slab | Original 0.20 m edge, left white | Existing `Wall_PaperWhite` |

All surfaces are inside the existing envelope; proud detail is 0 mm.
The full sill body, every snap face and all source dimensions are intact.
The column-base and sill finishes are flush material assignments, not added geometry over snap contacts.
The 20 mm difference between the street planes of sill and pier is the source kit's existing column proud dimension.

## Assessment

The strongest instant colour read of the three, and the least invasive geometry proposal.
It spends much more cobalt on the repeatable base architecture, so Alex would be approving that larger palette commitment as well as a new material slot.
The dark suits remain near-black through the clear upper panes; the spandrel is deliberately opaque cover.
Its glossy reflections are confined to the opaque band; the playable clear glass is nonreflective in these studies.
