# 03 / Split Blades

**Status:** Culled concept study · **Last updated:** 2026-11-11

Two sharp white fins replace the flat face of every pier, with a deep dark void between them.

## The large move

The 0.40 m column is carved 180 mm deep, leaving a continuous 60 mm rear web.
A central slot is 140 mm wide at its bed; each side fin occupies 130 mm at its root and tapers to a 55 mm street tip.
The angled faces make the opening wider at the street plane and catch opposite values in raking light.
The split runs from Y 0.055 to 4.245 m, including below the sill line, so the columns interrupt the floor bands as vertical pins.
Full 55 mm top and bottom caps preserve the original flat contacts.
The outside side face of each fin remains a full-depth flat snap face.

The street top of the sill is cut into one broad 200 mm-high, 120 mm-deep chamfer.
The slope occupies Y 0.80–1.00 m and ends at Unity Z -0.12 m at the top.
A continuous 80 mm opaque rear thickness remains at the narrowest point.
Untouched 40 mm end lands retain the full original sill end planes beneath the columns.

## Build dimensions and materials

| Part | Treatment | Material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pier fins | 180 mm depth, 55 mm street tips; 140 mm slot at the rear bed | Existing `Wall_PaperWhite` |
| Pier web | 60 mm continuous backing; slot bed in Deep Navy | White rear face; proposed `Trim_Navy` on slot bed |
| Corner fins | Same 140 mm slot, slightly wider roots within the original 0.42 m corner column | Same slots as facade pier |
| Sill | 200 mm-high broad chamfer; 120 mm cutback; full-height opaque backing | Existing `Wall_PaperWhite` |
| Rails | Original 50 × 100 mm section, unchanged positions | A: existing `Frame_Aluminium`; B: proposed `Trim_Navy` |
| Pane | Original clear pane, with no added mullion | Existing `Glass_Clear` slot, concept shader |

Proud detail is 0 mm; original outer extents, floor/soffit contacts and run contacts remain in place.
Carving is visual only: original column and sill box colliders remain the production requirement.
The rails and glass do not move into the recesses.

## Assessment

The strongest close-up profile and the most sculptural pier, but not distinct enough in the rival thumbnail.
At 300 px its paired vertical edges still read too much like White Trays, so it was cut from the shortlist under the round-3 rule.
Use the raw close-up to inspect the depth; the room face intentionally stays a plain white column.
No review boards were composed for this discarded study.
