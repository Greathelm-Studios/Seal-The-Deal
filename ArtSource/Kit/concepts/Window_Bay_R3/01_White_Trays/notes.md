# 01 / White Trays

**Status:** Target concept for Alex's review · **Last updated:** 2026-11-11

The rival sees broad white shelves floating in a dark opening; inside, the room remains Paper White.

## The large move

The street-side upper 160 mm of the sill is cut back by 120 mm.
The remaining 840 mm white sill face joins the unchanged 200 mm slab edge into a 1.04 m white body beneath the dark top reveal.
The silhouette comes from this whole-floor white mass, not a decorative score.

Above the sill, each pier's street face retreats 160 mm, from Unity Z +0.02 to -0.14 m, between Y 1.05 and 4.16 m.
The recessed face is Deep Navy, allowing the openings to read as a single dark band.
Full-depth 20 mm lands remain at each side contact; the top 140 mm and base through 1.05 m retain the full column envelope.
These lands are flush finish regions, not applied skins.

## Build dimensions and materials

| Part | Treatment | Material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sill | Original 4/2 m run, 0.20 m deep, 1.00 m high; continuous 80 mm opaque rear backing | Existing `Wall_PaperWhite` |
| Sill recess | Y 0.84–1.00 m; 120 mm depth; 40 mm untouched end lands hidden beneath columns | Proposed `Trim_Navy` on the recess bed |
| Pier | 160 mm street setback above sill; no change to room face, flat side contacts, floor or soffit contacts | White body; proposed `Trim_Navy` on visible street inset/lands |
| Rails | Original 0.05 m high × 0.10 m deep, at Y 1.00–1.05 and 4.25–4.30 m | A: existing `Frame_Aluminium`; B: proposed `Trim_Navy` |
| Pane | Original 3.60/1.60 × 3.20 × 0.02 m; no added bars | Existing slot `Glass_Clear`, separate concept shader |
| Slab edge | Unchanged 0.20 m white context slab | Existing `Wall_PaperWhite` |

All recesses stay inside the source bounds; proud detail is 0 mm.
Sills have no perforations and remain opaque over the full metre of cover height.
Original box colliders, object splits, pivots and snap planes are retained as design constraints; no collider or production asset was authored here.
The same section shortens to a 2 m infill; the handed corner columns keep the source extents shown on the corner sheet.

## Assessment

**Recommended direction, with B navy rails.**
The white shelf / dark opening rhythm reads across the street and feels closest to a confident 2001 corporate rendering.
It also keeps the largest mass white and leaves the entire clear opening free of extra framing.
The price is a visibly thinner sill in its recessed upper strip: the opaque backing is 80 mm; the original full box collider must remain unchanged.
This is a visual mesh proposal for review, not approval to change gameplay cover or kit geometry.
