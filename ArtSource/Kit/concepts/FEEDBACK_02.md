## What "further" means

**One idea per direction that you can see from 12 m and recognise in a thumbnail.** Work at the scale of the whole column, the whole sill, the whole slab edge: 100–200 mm moves, not millimetres.
The envelope rules still hold (recesses unlimited, ≤ 15 mm proud, snap faces fixed, sill solid). Use them: a 0.4 × 0.24 m column and a 1 m × 0.2 m sill are big enough to carve hard.

Starting points, bigger than last time (mix, beat or ignore them):

- **White trays.** The street face of the sill plus the slab edge read as one thick white band per floor, with a deep shadow recess (100 mm+) where the sill meets the glass, so the tower reads as stacked white trays floating over dark glass. The classic turn-of-the-millennium render look.
- **Spandrel glass.** The street face of the sill is a flush back-painted glass panel in Deep Navy or Cobalt, so each floor shows one tall clear band and one dark glossy band. "Dark window bands" from bible §09, done in glass.
- **Blade columns.** Each 0.4 m column is carved into a sharp profile: deep chamfers to a narrow street face, or split into two fins with a dark slot between, so the columns read as vertical blades in raking light.
- **Light line.** A continuous recessed cyan or white line in the head (the 0.05 m rail zone or the column heads), running across the whole façade, so a floor reads as a lit stripe from the rival tower. Cyan only as a thin line (bible §03).
- **Clean curve.** Corner columns with a large radius cut into the outer corner (as large as the envelope allows), so the building corner reads as one architectural curve. Not bubbly.

**Then pick a winner yourself:** of what you try, present the **best two or three**, and say which one you'd build and why.

## Staging, which matters as much as the design

Render each direction in a scene that sells it:

- **Sun along the street**, strong cobalt sky, pale haze, a simple white-and-blue block city behind (large blocks, no detail).
- **The rival's view:** three bays × three floors from 12 m away at the same floor height, sunlit, with **two dark navy suits** standing in the room and one on the floor above. **The suits must read as near-black through two panes**: glass ~5% opacity, no milky film, bright Paper White interior behind them.
- **The room view:** eye height 1.7 m, three bays, grey-blue carpet tiles and a white ceiling-tile grid with strip lights (placeholders are fine), city through the glass.
- **A close three-quarter of one column and sill** at 2–3 m, raking light, so the profile reads.
- **A thumbnail test:** each direction's rival view shrunk to 300 px wide on one strip. If two directions look alike at that size, one of them isn't a direction.
- Keep one dimensioned turnaround page per direction (front, side, section, three-quarter), shaded, with the real sizes.
