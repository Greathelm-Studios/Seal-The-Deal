## The look

The same rules as `concepts/Props_Aero/REQUEST.md`: moulded, pillowed forms with generous radii; pearl-white gloss with brushed silver and chrome hardware; frosted aqua acrylic; smoked glass; cyan LED dot-matrix; the AONAGI ring (open aqua ring, red hanko dot at the lower-right gap) and short bilingual labels only where a real lift carries them. Colours: pearl `#F4F8F8`, silver `#C8D2D9`, aqua `#45BFC7`, frosted aqua `#C6E9E6`/`#8FDCE0`, mist `#A8BFD1`, cobalt `#244FB9` (small), navy `#111B38`, hanko red `#C7473E` (logo dot only). No bubbles, no plants in the lift, no vaporwave.

A 2003 Japanese office lift feels like a precision product: a softly radiused silver portal, a smoked indicator with cyan dot-matrix floor codes and arrows, round touch buttons with cyan rings, a hairline-brushed car with a mirror-silver back wall, a glowing frosted ceiling, a rounded handrail, a floor of pale stone or mist resin. Readable at a glance in a fast shooter: the doors and the indicator are the landmarks.
