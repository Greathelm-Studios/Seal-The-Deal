# Office concept directions

**Status** Target concepts for Alex's review

**Last updated** 2026-11-12

A bright, slightly empty corporate future frames dark-suited lawyers, a clear spiral stair and one red locked gate.

Start with the [contact sheet](contact_sheet.png), with one row per direction and the four requested views in reading order.

All twelve paintings were made with the built-in image generation tool.

## Shared construction proposals

The [kit README](../../README.md) supplies the construction dimensions and existing piece names.

The [request](REQUEST.md) supplies the concept layout.

The reference floor-plan sketch depicts an older 20 m-wide floor, a 10 m main area and rear stairs.

The request and current blockout govern the 28 m-wide floor and centre-back spiral used here.

| Element | Construction target |
| --- | --- |
| Floor | 28 m wide, 14 m open main floor and 4 m rear service spine. |
| Front | Seven 4 m bays with 1 m opaque cover sills and uninterrupted clear glass above. |
| Heights | 4.3 m clear soffit, 4.5 m floor spacing and 0.2 m slab. |
| Stair | Centre about 10 m behind the façade, one full turn per floor, treads out to 2.6 m radius. |
| Centre column | 0.8 m radius, following the kit's explicit measurement. |
| Cage | Tall flat clear panels at the kit's 2.75 m radius, with an open arrival gap. |
| Meeting rooms | 2.5 m high clear partitions, open above to the ceiling. |
| Service rooms | Three opaque rooms, WC / STORE / UTIL, with 1.2 × 2.4 m door openings. |
| Rival | Matching floor heights and façade rhythm across the 12 m street gap. |

I interpreted the request's “0.8 m white column” using the kit's explicit radius.

The kit's suggested 8 / 12 / 8 m service-room split guided the cutaways.

The roof and front glazing are selectively cut away in the dollhouse paintings to expose the layout.

### Stair finishes and gate

**Proposed `Stair_PaperWhite` slot** gives `Stair_Column` its white finish, while `Stair_Flight` uses `Stair_Aluminium`.

**Proposed `Door_LockedRed` slot** reserves the strong red finish for the stair's closed gate.

**Proposed `Stair_Gate_Radial` visual variant** places the hinge at the centre column and the latch at the outer cage, just after the arrival gap.

The radial walking width is approximately 1.8 m from the 2.6 m tread radius minus the 0.8 m column radius, before frame allowances.

The existing `Door_Leaf` is 1.18 m wide, so the gate needs its own reviewed visual variant to span that path.

The paintings explore a sloping gate silhouette that follows the stair composition.

Final gate framing and leaf shape are an approval item for the selected direction.

All proposed slots and pieces are concept-stage production suggestions.

## Resolution and visual review

The tool returned every painting at 1672 × 941 despite requests for the largest native landscape output.

Each final image is 1664 × 936, exact 16:9, using a centred crop of four pixels on each side, two at the top and three at the bottom.

The images retain their native pixel scale.

The contact sheet is 3960 × 2088 and uses twelve 960 × 540 thumbnails.

The sheet was composed with PowerShell and System.Drawing.

All twelve delivered paintings and the assembled sheet were visually inspected.

The review covered the central stair, three-room spine, open meeting-room glazing, cover sills, coarse bay divisions, red gate, suit visibility and the absence of decorative plants.

### Visual limitations

The paintings' carpet has more visible mottling than the bible's low-noise material target.

**Suggestion** use a restrained grey-blue carpet material with subtle tile seams when the selected direction is modelled.

Painted perspective, tread spacing, gate framing and glass-panel faceting vary between views.

Exact radii, camera height, street distance and floor spacing need metric verification against the kit during modelling.

The generated clear-glass appearance provides a visual target for a future material.

Unity material response and cross-tower gameplay readability require direct observation in the game.

### Static file verification

The following PowerShell checks ran from the repository root.

```powershell
Add-Type -AssemblyName System.Drawing
$officeConceptFolders = @('01_White_Trays','02_Cobalt_Portal','03_Luminous_Grid')
$officeAssetCount = 0
$officeInvalidCount = 0
foreach ($officeConceptFolder in $officeConceptFolders) {
    foreach ($officeShotName in @('cutaway','main_floor','rival_view','stair')) {
        $officeFile = Get-Item -LiteralPath ('ArtSource/Kit/concepts/Office/' + $officeConceptFolder + '/' + $officeShotName + '.png')
        $officeAsset = [System.Drawing.Image]::FromFile($officeFile.FullName)
        if ($officeAsset.Width -ne 1664 -or $officeAsset.Height -ne 936 -or $officeAsset.Width * 9 -ne $officeAsset.Height * 16) { $officeInvalidCount++ }
        $officeAssetCount++
        $officeAsset.Dispose()
    }
}
$officeSheetAsset = [System.Drawing.Image]::FromFile((Resolve-Path -LiteralPath 'ArtSource/Kit/concepts/Office/contact_sheet.png'))
Write-Output "Concept images $officeAssetCount, invalid sizes $officeInvalidCount, contact sheet $($officeSheetAsset.Width)x$($officeSheetAsset.Height)"
$officeSheetAsset.Dispose()
$officeArchive = Get-Content -Encoding UTF8 -LiteralPath 'ArtSource/Kit/concepts/Office/generation_prompts.json' -Raw | ConvertFrom-Json
Write-Output "Prompt records $($officeArchive.prompts.Count), image records $($officeArchive.images.Count), summed tries $(($officeArchive.images | Measure-Object -Property tries -Sum).Sum)"
```

Observed results were 12 concept images, zero invalid sizes, a 3960 × 2088 contact sheet, 20 prompt records, 12 image records and 20 summed tries.

## Which direction I would build

I would build **White Trays**.

Its broad white bands and navy reveals give the strongest architectural identity while keeping the office pale and the suited silhouettes readable.

It extends the latest window study through existing kit families and lets the clear spiral and red gate hold the centre of the floor.
