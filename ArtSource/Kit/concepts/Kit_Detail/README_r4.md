## Direction and the whole floor

[FEEDBACK_03.md](FEEDBACK_03.md), including its Style section, governs this pass wherever older briefs or the bible's general style emphasis differ.
The added Frutiger Aero influence is architectural: glossy white, rounded negative cuts, sky-filled transparency, high frosted inserts and soft light within a restrained blue/white/silver/navy system.
Gen X Soft Club supplies negative space, thin rules, spaced light typography and sky imagery; hard sun, deep cavities and room-scale cobalt retain round 3's boldness.
No greenery, bubbles, droplets, pink chrome, pastel/rainbow gradients or nostalgia props were added.

The four bible references, all three office plates, the art-bible text, the round-3 contact sheet, README and exact prompts were reviewed first.
The approved White Trays / Cobalt Portal paintings and the existing Unity office captures were also inspected in [source_review_r4.png](source_review_r4.png).
Bible references 01–03 informed the era language, 04 the massing, and the office-floor and atrium plates the open room, office materials, sky and depth.

**A means the round-3 pick refined for the interior**, regardless of its former letter: façade A, walls A, stair B, ceiling A and partition B become round-4 A.
**B means a new interior-led alternative.**
The play views bring the room-height floor index, spiral stair, cobalt carpet route and white meeting enclosure into the same room, with the active family leading the composition.
The stair B capsule replaces the helix on that core; it is an alternative, not a second layer over the helix.

**Proposed build mix: façade B, walls A, stair A, ceiling A, partition B.**
This keeps direct floor and route cues while using the capsule bay and high skyglass lintel to make the era visible throughout the office.
These are recommendations for review, not approved changes to the kit or the office decision.

## Fixed boundaries and depth convention

All dimensions below are **millimetres**.
**Recessed** means cut inward from the piece's original exposed face; **flush** means 0 mm proud.
Every new move below is recessed or flush: **new proud geometry is 0 mm throughout**, beneath the allowed 15 mm maximum.
White islands between carved numeral strokes remain at the original wall plane; they must never become attached, raised white numerals.
Likewise the helix is a groove in the core, never a ribbon projecting from it.

Keep the existing envelopes, pivots, flat snap lands, floor/soffit contacts and all colliders.
Use the existing 4,000 bay, 600 façade sill, 4,300 floor-to-soffit, 4,500 storey and 200 slab.
Service openings stay 1,200 × 2,400; the actual silver leaf remains 1,180 × 2,380 × 40 with its hinge pivot unchanged.
Façade columns remain 400 × 240 × 4,300; corner columns retain their 420 width.
Stair core / tread / rail / cage radii remain 800 / 2,600 / 2,630 / 2,750.
The full-height red stair leaf stays 1,750 × 4,200 × 200, with the existing hardware allowance retained.

Partition runs remain 4,000 / 2,000 × 100 deep × 2,500 high, leaving **1,800 open air above** to the office ceiling.
No roof or raised room floor is part of either partition direction.
All partition glass from 450 to 2,100 stays near-clear and unobstructed; opaque/diffuse upper treatments start at 2,100 or higher.
The lower frame is a narrow vertical band at the pane footprint, never a platform below the room.

The existing [glass specification](../../../../docs/systems/GLASS.md) remains authoritative: one broad and one thin translucent diagonal streak, no actual mirror reflection.
Sky sheen and gloss are visual proposals concentrated at grazing angles and on opaque finishes; the clear transmission region must still reveal dark suits through two panes.
High frosted glass in partition B is a separate proposed material, not a replacement for the clear gameplay panes.
Keep the original colliders over the original bounds: these reliefs do not add cover, change the walking surface or change any collision outcome.
Do not combine opposing recesses through a thin piece; preserve the minimum skins listed below and the original snap/contact lands.

- **Pier:** relocate the round-3 folded pocket to the inner face: **recessed 160**, 280-wide cobalt back, from height 660 to 4,160, retaining 20 side lands and the 140 head land; inner transition radius 40 is cut inward.

**B — Capsule-cut sky bays turn the window line into an air terminal.**

- **Pieces:** the same columns and inner window finishes, with the original pane planes and rectangular openings retained.

- **2001-future cue:** tall capsule cavities, softly sky-lit gloss and spaced department labels recall optimistic air-terminal/product design rather than a present-day curtain wall.
- **Play read:** rounded inner relief changes the light while leaving the crossing lane, sill cover and window sightline intact.

**Materials:** existing `Wall_PaperWhite`, `Accent_Cobalt`, `Trim_Navy`, `Glass_Clear`; proposals `Finish_WhiteGloss`, `Metal_BrushedSilver`, and B's `Light_CoveWhite` with a thin cyan accent.
**Build B:** its inner capsules carry the requested era language into every window-side play view without designing a new exterior façade.

## Material proposals, not new approved assets

| --- | --- |
| `Finish_WhiteGloss` | Paper White satin/gloss finish on the specified carved opaque surfaces; shader/material variation, not added mesh thickness. |
