# Kit detail, round 4: prioritise the interior

**From:** Alex, drafted by Claude · **Date:** 2026-11-12 · Read with [FEEDBACK_02.md](FEEDBACK_02.md) and [README_r3.md](README_r3.md). This brief wins where they differ.

## Alex's verdict on round 3

**Keep the round-3 boldness: carved form, storey-scale cobalt, hard light. Now prioritise the interior.** Players spend the match inside the office floor. That is where the kit has to be memorable, readable and good to move through. The street read was given equal weight in round 3, so half of every sheet went on it. It now comes second.

## What changes

1. **Interior first.** Every direction is designed and judged from inside the floor at 1.7 m eye height. Give each direction **two interior views**:
   - a **hero** view: layered and sunlit, a strong diagonal and foreground mass, as in round 3
   - a **play** view from a different spot. It shows how the move reads while crossing the floor: where it leads the eye, what it tells a player about route, floor or cover, and what it does to the sightlines.
2. **Street view demoted.** Keep a small inset of the 12 m rival view, about a quarter of the panel, only to show the move doesn't break the three-floor read. Don't design for it.
3. **Families, re-weighted.** Spend the effort on the interior families: **walls and door, stair, ceiling and floor, glass partition**. Treat the **façade only as seen from inside**: the inner face of the piers and head beam, the window bay, the sill, and how the sun comes through. Its outer face only matters in the inset.
4. **The room as a whole.** At least one view per family shows the move next to the other families' round-3 picks, so the floor reads as one designed space: the room-height floor number, the stair spiral, the cobalt route and the carved meeting box together. Don't make five separate showpieces.
5. **Interior light.** Sun from the façade raking deep into the floor, hard window-mullion shadows across carpet and white walls, cool bounce, bloom on sunlit edges, darker value toward the core. The interior must not go flat-grey away from the windows.
6. **Directions.** A = the round-3 pick for that family refined for the interior. B = a new interior-led idea. Keep both as bold as round 3.

## Style: lean harder into the era

Alex also wants round 4 to **lean further into Frutiger Aero, 2000s corporate futurism and Gen X Soft Club**. Round 3 is bold but reads as a generic modern office with big graphics. Make it unmistakably *a multinational's 2001 vision of the future* (art bible §1 and the secondary references). Study `refs/bible_ref_01.png`–`03.png` for this, not only plate 04.

- **2000s corporate futurism:** optimistic, brand-managed, slightly artificial. Clean architectural curves and radiused corners next to the hard geometry: rounded-rectangle openings, capsule-shaped light coves, curved glass returns, elliptical ceiling cut-outs, a curved reception or meeting pod. Satin and gloss white like early-2000s consumer tech and airport lounges. Brushed silver trim. Thin sans-serif corporate signage with lots of spacing, logo-like marks, floor and department codes.
- **Frutiger Aero, the architectural kind:** glossy, luminous, sky-filled. Strong sky reflections and soft specular highlights on glass and gloss surfaces. Translucent frosted-glass panels with a soft cyan-to-white glow. Soft gradients on light coves and signage. A clean, aspirational, airy feel, with bloom and light wrapping round edges.
- **Gen X Soft Club:** air, negative space, restraint, transparency. Pale layered surfaces, thin hairline grids and rules, tiny technical microtype, sky imagery printed or framed as part of the wall, cool editorial calm. Fewer objects, more space.
- **How far:** the art bible rules still apply. **No greenery, bubbles, water droplets, rainbow or pastel gradients, pink chrome or vaporwave.** Curves are clean and architectural, never blobby. Blue, white, silver and navy stay the structural colours; cyan stays the thin accent. The era comes from form, material, light and typography, not from props or nostalgia.
- In `README_r4.md`, add a line per direction saying what makes it read as 2001-future and not as a 2025 office.
