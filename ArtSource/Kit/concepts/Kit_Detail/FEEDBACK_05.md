## The game changed

**It is no longer a sniper war between twin towers.** The game is now **one massive office, laid out like a labyrinth, designed for fast close-quarters combat**. Think of the pace and map logic of a fast movement-shooter arena like STRAFTAT: tight loops, short sightlines that open into a few longer ones, lots of corners and doorways, routes that branch and rejoin, and verticality you can move through quickly. Use STRAFTAT only as a reference for **gameplay pacing and map flow**. Don't copy its look, layouts or assets; the look is ours (the art bible plus FEEDBACK_04).

## What the kit has to do now

The kit builds a maze-like office floor plate. Design every piece for **combat readability and flow**:

1. **Corridors and junctions:** walls, corners, T- and X-junctions, doorways. Players fight round corners, so make corner treatments distinct and readable, for example a rounded glossy corner, a cobalt return or a glass corner.
2. **Rooms and arenas:** small rooms off corridors, and a few larger open halls or atriums as fight hubs, with clean cover shapes (low glossy counters, capsule desks, pillars) at useful heights: about 1.0–1.2 m for crouch cover, full height for hard cover.
3. **Glass as gameplay:** glass walls show you where enemies are without letting you walk through. Mix solid, clear-glass and frosted-glass walls so the player always knows what they can see and shoot through. Glass is breakable in the game (the shooting lane is adding it), so a glass wall is a route that can open. Keep glass near-clear with the two-streak sheen, never mirrored.
4. **Navigation in a maze:** players must never get lost. Every zone needs a **landmark and a colour/graphic identity**: big department codes, zone numbers or letters set into walls and floors, cobalt fields marking a zone, light lines on the floor or ceiling leading to stairs and hubs. Each direction should show how a player finds their way.
5. **Verticality:** stairs, the spiral stair, mezzanines, glass balconies over an atrium, drop-downs, ramps. Movement is fast, so vertical links should be wide, readable and quick to take.
6. **Floors and ceilings:** polished glossy floors (no carpet) that reflect light and figures. Clean ceiling planes with long light lines or coves that also point along routes.
