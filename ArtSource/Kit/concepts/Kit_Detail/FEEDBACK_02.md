# Kit detail, round 3: push it further

**From:** Alex, drafted by Claude · **Date:** 2026-11-12 · Read with [FEEDBACK_01.md](FEEDBACK_01.md) and [README_r2.md](README_r2.md). This brief wins where they differ.

## Alex's verdict on round 2

**Better, and on direction, but we can push it more.** Round 2 has the right colours and light. But most of the ideas are stickers: a flat number, a stripe, a printed band, a carpet tint applied to the same boxy white room. Put the round-2 sheets next to `refs/bible_ref_04.png` and the difference is obvious. Plate 04 is built from **masses**: cantilevered volumes, stepped and folded façades, whole faces in cobalt, giant Z and diagonal shapes that wrap corners, curved bands. Its contrast runs from bright white to deep shadow and blue-black glass. Round 2 is a polite office with graphics on it.

## What "more" means

1. **Form before paint.** Every direction must change the **silhouette or the light** of the piece, not just its colour: deep carved reveals, chamfered or folded planes, stepped returns, soffits that tilt, columns that taper or split, recesses that catch a hard shadow line. You can still use paint, but on top of a form move, not instead of one. The constraint is unchanged: recesses as deep as you like; proud detail at most 15 mm. Carve *in*.
2. **Scale up 2–3×.** Round 2's graphics are 150–900 mm wide. Plate 04's are storey-sized. A cobalt field should own a whole column face, a whole wall bay or a whole stair cage panel, and wrap the corner onto the return. A floor number should be as tall as the room is high. The test: **the idea must still read in a 200 px thumbnail.**
3. **Ration harder, so it hits harder.** One huge cobalt move per view beats three medium ones. White still dominates the frame (about 60–70 %). Cobalt is the second voice and a big one (about 15–25 % of the feature, not 3 %). Cyan stays a single thin line.
4. **Harder light and deeper values.** Low raking sun, long hard shadow shapes across white planes and carpet, true near-black in the glass reflections and the shadowed reveals, bloom on sunlit edges, atmospheric haze in depth. If a view has no shadow edge you can trace, relight it.
5. **More dramatic cameras.** Keep the 1.7 m hero eye height, but compose like plate 04: a strong diagonal, a near foreground mass cutting the frame, a deep view to the rival tower. Avoid square-on walls. The rival view from 12 m must show the move changing the whole three-floor read of the tower, not a small detail on it.
6. **Be bolder in B.** For each family, **A = round 2's strongest idea pushed to its maximum** (bigger, carved, wrapping), and **B = something new and wilder**, one notch past what you think is safe. Alex would rather pull a concept back than push one forward again.

## Starting points per family (to beat, not copy)

1. **Façade:** A, the cobalt knife piers become full-depth carved fins that fold out of the column and wrap onto the head beam, a storey-tall cobalt face per bay, stacked into a vertical rhythm up the tower. B, the façade reads as stepped slabs: each floor's head beam carved into a deep shadow slot with the cyan line inside, so the tower reads as stacked white bands from across the street.
2. **Walls and door:** A, the floor number is as tall as the room, partly carved into the wall as a deep recess and partly cobalt, wrapping round the spine wall's corner. B, a cobalt Z or diagonal at storey scale that runs over the wall, the portal head and onto the ceiling, so the whole portal reads as one graphic mass.
3. **Stair:** A, the red lock panel becomes a full-height graphic slab (the red door is the hero: deep lock symbol, carved hazard rule). B, the stair cage itself becomes the landmark: a cobalt helix ribbon wrapping the full height of the cage glass or core, readable through three floors from the rival tower.
4. **Ceiling and floor:** A, the cobalt carpet route becomes a floor-to-ceiling route: carpet, a carved soffit slot above it with the light line, and a cobalt wall return where it meets the stair. B, a deep tilted or coffered soffit that throws shadow and frames the view to the city.
5. **Glass partition:** A, the corner brackets become storey-scale cobalt corner posts or folded frames, solid above 2.1 m and below 0.45 m, with knee-to-head kept clear. B, a meeting room as a white carved box with a cobalt interior face visible through the glass, as a volume rather than a print.
