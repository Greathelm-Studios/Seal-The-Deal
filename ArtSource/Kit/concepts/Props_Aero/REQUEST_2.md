## Sheet 5 — computers (`sheet_5_computers.png`)

The 2001–2004 Japanese office computer, imagined slightly forward:
- **Monitor_Flat** (loose, thrown in fights): early flat panel, silver or pearl bezel 20–30 mm with a soft radius, a smoked dark screen with a faint reflection streak, a round or capsule foot with a chrome neck, a small cyan power LED, the AONAGI ring as a tiny badge on the bezel. Panel 0.55 × 0.34 m; about 0.46 m high overall. Show one alternative: a **compact deep-back pearl CRT-style monitor** with the same footprint feel, as option B.
- **PC_Tower** (new loose prop, about 0.19 × 0.42 × 0.40 m): a translucent aqua or frosted-milk side panel over a pearl body, a silver front with a drive slot, a round power button with a cyan ring, rounded vents. It sits on the floor beside the pedestal.
- **Keyboard** (loose): pale grey-white, 0.44 × 0.14, 15–30 mm tall, a soft wedge with a silver or aqua trim strip; keys as a texture grid, not geometry. A pale **mouse** (about 0.06 × 0.11 × 0.035) on an aqua pad.
