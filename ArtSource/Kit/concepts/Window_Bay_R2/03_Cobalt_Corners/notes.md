# 03 — Cobalt Corners

**Idea:** Large diagonal cobalt fields identify the tower corners while ordinary repeating bays stay white.

| Detail | Size / placement | Material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Corner graphic | Corner columns only, room and street faces; diagonal polygon spans Y 0.50–3.45, with 0.65 rise across column width; 1 mm finish skin | Proposed `Accent_Cobalt` #244FB9 |
| Ordinary pier axial mark | 0.060 wide on standard 0.40 pier; Y 0.20–4.10, street face, 1 mm finish skin | Proposed `Trim_Navy` |
| Pier edge treatment | 0.018 radius cut inward on visible edges; preserve flat production snap lands | `Wall_PaperWhite` |
| Street base anchor | Y 0–0.18, runs across sill only; 1 mm finish skin, no alteration to bottom snap face | Proposed `Trim_Navy` |
| Silver sill fold | 0.015 high, Y 0.985–1.00, inside original solid sill | Existing `Frame_Aluminium` |

**Placement rule proposed for Alex:** cobalt only on CornerL and CornerR, never every intermediate column.
The 3-bay hero/street facade therefore has two graphic end piers and two white internal piers.
The 2 m infill page shows both corner profiles together as a small corner-detail study; it is not the proposed colour frequency for a long facade.
The diagonal is a graphic finish on the narrow pier, not a diagonal mullion across the pane.
Rail A/B is independent of the corner colour.

Proposed new slots: **Accent_Cobalt**, **Trim_Navy**.
