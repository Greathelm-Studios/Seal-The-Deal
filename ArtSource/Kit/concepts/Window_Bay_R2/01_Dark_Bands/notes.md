# 01 — Dark Bands

**Idea:** The street facade reads as long navy belts interrupted by narrow white piers with deep vertical scores.

| Detail | Size / placement | Material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paired pier scores | Each 0.040 wide, 0.060 deep from room/street face; start 0.12 above floor, finish 0.12 below soffit | White cheeks in `Wall_PaperWhite`; recess bed proposes `Trim_Navy` |
| White side lands | 0.035 wide outside each score; white central face 0.25 on standard pier | `Wall_PaperWhite` |
| Street sill belt | Y 0.80–1.00; continuous across sill and column street faces; 1 mm finish skin | Proposed `Trim_Navy` |
| Street head rhythm | Rail remains 0.05 high at opening; pier's navy head marker Y 4.10–4.30 | Proposed `Trim_Navy`; rail A/B independent |
| Sill top | 0.018 high finish volume within top of original solid sill | Proposed `Trim_Navy` |
| Pier edge character | Crisp white lands around square-cut reveals; no added projection | `Wall_PaperWhite` |

The head belt is deliberately only rail-height across the pane: widening it downward would eat into the fixed clear opening.
The stronger 0.20 m horizontal move therefore sits below the sill line, retaining the full standing-silhouette view.
The dark bands are part of this direction in both rail comparisons; A/B changes only the two narrow rails.
Corner profiles use the kit's actual handed extents; reveal treatment repeats on them without moving their pivots.
The 2 m infill uses the same section and 1.60 m clear pane.

Proposed new slot: **Trim_Navy**; no light or cobalt material needed.
