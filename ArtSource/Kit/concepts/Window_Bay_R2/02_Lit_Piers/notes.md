# 02 — Lit Piers

**Idea:** The room is paced by luminous white piers, with a fine floating silver sill instead of a dark street belt.

| Detail | Size / placement | Material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Room-face slot | 0.060 wide × 0.065 deep; Y 0.12–4.18, preserving end lands | `Wall_PaperWhite` cheeks; proposed `Trim_Navy` bed |
| Recessed luminous ribbon | 0.018 wide, Y 0.18–4.12; about 0.061 behind pier face | Proposed `Light_Strip`, pale white emission |
| Soft pier cheeks | 0.018 visible edge radius, cut into original pier envelope | `Wall_PaperWhite` |
| Silver sill cap | 0.025 high, Y 0.975–1.00; full 0.20 sill depth, inside the original envelope | Existing `Frame_Aluminium` |
| Shadow under cap | 0.012 high, Y 0.947–0.959; 1 mm finish skin room and street side | Proposed `Trim_Navy` |

The light is on the room face only: the rival sees a quiet white structure and the chosen rail finish.
It is a recessed white architectural light, not coloured neon or a bar across a player.
Keep the visual cap silver in A and B; only the actual 0.05 m rails change finish.
The radius must stop at flat snap lands in a production model; the concept bevel is a visual treatment study.
CornerL/R and 2 m infill retain the source extents and use the same centred slot treatment.

Proposed new slots: **Trim_Navy**, **Light_Strip**.
This carries an emissive-material proposal only, with no runtime light, bloom or performance requirement implied.
