# KitOffice satire: posters and pitch deck

Deadpan, corporate and dry: the joke is that it looks exactly like a real motivational poster or a real deck. No swearing, no real companies or people. AONAGI is the landlord; the two law firms (Ashcombe & Pryce, Halloran Voss) lease the floor.

## Wall text (Alex, 2026-11-15: "texts that wrap around the wall"; "one per wall, the wrapping is confusing")

Big lettering in a stripe high on the core walls: one whole message per wall face, never split round a corner. "face" is
the face's index in `markings/l2_on_kit.json` decals.wallText: core 0 = the 4 m court face, core 1 = the 6 m window-hall
face, core 2 and core 3 = the two 8 m corridor faces (over the cobalt field, then the white wall), south 0 = the 6 m
south end. Keep a message short enough for its wall; the generator shrinks letters that would overrun. Sets: firm A's half
