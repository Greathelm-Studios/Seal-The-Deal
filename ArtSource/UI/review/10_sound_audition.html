SEAL THE DEAL · sound audition

Each slot says where its sound came from: synthesised (made by the generator), recorded (a cleared library recording, levelled to the same loudness) or missing. As recordings arrive, the same buttons play them, so the two can be compared.

The synthesised set was judged too artificial; recordings are being sourced. Start with the walk: it plays the menu the way a player meets it in their first minute, with the ambient bed under the title. Then play any group on its own. Note any that are harsh, too long, too alike, or wrong for what the player just did.
