| [04_gallery_sun_slab_on_floor](light_and_grade/04_gallery_sun_slab_on_floor.png) | A white gallery in blue shade, one bright slab of sun across a glossy floor. | The look of a single sun patch in a blue room. |

These are what `OfficeLook.cs` was regraded toward: a sun that crosses the street and casts hard shadows, a pale blue ambient so shade reads blue and sunlit white stays white, a cyan-blue colour filter, lifted shadows, strong bloom and faint film grain.
