**Alex's verdict on round 1: "they look too toyish."** All three looked like water pistols or toy-shop guns.

## What made round 1 toyish (fix all of these)

- **Stubby proportions.** Short, tall, fat slides and a squat grip. A real service pistol's slide is long and low: about 5.5 times as long as it is tall.
- **Pillowy radii everywhere.** Big soft corners on every edge. Real pistols have crisp, machined edges (1–2 mm chamfers), flat slide flanks and a few deliberate curves (the beavertail, the trigger guard, the grip's back).
- **Oversized, blob-like parts.** Huge sights, a huge trigger and a huge guard. Use real-size parts.
- **Glossy plastic everywhere and flat colour blocks.** Use real finishes: satin or bead-blasted steel, nitride black, textured matte polymer and a darker barrel. Gloss only where a real gun is polished.
- **No mechanical detail.** Add the parts that say "this works": front and rear slide serrations, an ejection port with the barrel hood showing, an extractor, a slide-stop lever, a takedown lever, a magazine release, a recessed and crowned muzzle with the barrel visible inside the slide, a textured grip panel, a beavertail and a dust cover under the barrel (no rail).

**Keep it stylised for a toon-shaded game:** clean, readable shapes and no grime, wear, scratches or photo-real noise. Think of a **premium action-game weapon render**, believable and a little heroic, not a toy and not a photograph.

## Three options, one sheet

Three **different real-world pistol types**, each a believable full-size service pistol with no real brand:

- **A: Aero service pistol.** A modern striker-fired polymer pistol. Satin stainless slide, frame in a light warm grey-white matte polymer (not glossy pearl), aqua only as a **small** accent (a magazine-base inset or the front-sight dot) and a small engraved AONAGI ring on the slide. The office palette, grown up.
- **B: Duty black.** The same modern striker-fired type in nitride black steel and black textured polymer, with a gunmetal barrel showing in the port. The office colours appear only as a tiny aqua front-sight dot. The most serious option.
- **C: Executive classic.** A hammer-fired all-metal pistol, in the spirit of a classic double-action service pistol: an exposed hammer, a longer flat slide, a straight grip with **wood or dark-navy grip panels** and a small silver AONAGI medallion on each panel, in satin silver or two-tone silver and navy. The pistol a corporate security chief would carry.
