## What the reference does (the look to hit)

- **A gun drawn by an industrial designer, not a gunsmith.** It looks like a premium consumer product (a phone, an electric shaver, a camera) that happens to be a pistol.
- **Monolithic, sculpted body.** The slide and frame read as one smooth form with **few, deliberate shut lines**. Large flat or gently curved planes meet at crisp feature lines.
- **A sweeping feature line** runs from the muzzle along the side and down into the trigger guard, which is part of the frame's flowing shape.
- **Almost no mechanical detail:** no serrations, screws, levers, textured stippling or visible hammer. One small inset button or panel line at most.
- **One small glowing status light** (red in the reference) as the single bright accent.
- **Matte, satin finishes**, like an anodised or soft-touch product: not toy gloss and not gun-blue.
- Real proportions, as in round 2: a long, low top and a properly raked grip. The grip flares at the base and has a gentle palm swell.

This sits between rounds 1 and 2: as clean as round 1, as grown-up as round 2.

## Three options, one sheet

All three share that language and differ in form and colour:

- **A: Close to the reference.** A satin gunmetal-grey body, a slightly darker grip and an **aqua** status light where the reference has red. A small debossed AONAGI ring on the side.
- **B: Aero white.** The same idea in the office palette: a pearl-white or light-grey satin body, a brushed-silver top flat and feature line, an aqua status light and a frosted-aqua magazine base. It must look like a high-end 2002 Japanese gadget, **not a toy**: matte, crisp feature lines, no candy gloss.
- **C: Executive navy.** A sharper, more angular, faceted version (more planes and stronger edges) in deep navy `#111B38` with satin silver accents along the feature line, and a cobalt or aqua status light.

## Envelope (metres, length × width × height)

As round 2: about **0.20 × 0.032 × 0.14**. The grip is about 0.048 front-to-back × 0.032 across and **no thicker**, raked 14–18°. The trigger guard's inner opening is about 0.040 × 0.020. The slide (the top part) is still one separate part that recoils about 0.03, and the magazine one separate part from the grip base, even if their shut lines are subtle.
